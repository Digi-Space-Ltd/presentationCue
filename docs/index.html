PresentationCue

Your talking points, always on cue.

Prepare interview talking points or presentation speaker notes in sections, then keep every point on screen when it matters - on Zoom, Teams, Meet, or whilst you present.

Available for macOS and Windows.

Stay on camera. Cover every point.

It is easy to forget something you meant to say five minutes after a call or presentation ends. PresentationCue keeps your notes in a discreet overlay so you can glance, jump between sections, and keep eye contact.

What you can do

  • Organise talking points or speaker notes by section
  • Import PDF, PowerPoint speaker notes, or text with numbered headings
  • Press Play to load notes into a floating or pinned overlay
  • Hide the overlay from screen share when you need privacy
  • Collapse to a thin bar until you need the next cue
